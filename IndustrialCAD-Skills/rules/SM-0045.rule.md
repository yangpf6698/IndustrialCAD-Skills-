---
id: SM-0045
title: Minimum Hole Edge Distance / 最小孔边距
severity: ERROR
description: |
  EN: The minimum distance between the hole center and the free edge shall not be less than 1.5 × hole diameter.
  CN: 孔中心到自由边缘的最小距离不得小于孔直径的 1.5 倍。
validation:
  metric: EdgeDistance
  expression: "EdgeDistance >= 1.5 * Diameter"
notes:
  - Applies to sheet metal parts unless otherwise specified by material or process.
